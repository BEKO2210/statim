- As in run 1, RSS rises during the first two to three hours while the allocator and engine pools
  fill, then stays flat. From hour 2 on, the least-squares slope is **+0.37 MiB per hour**.
- Threads stayed at 45–46 and open file descriptors at 5–6 after start-up.
- The server log (access log off) has 7,362 lines, all at level `info`: start-up, adapter loading,
  cancelled inferences and the shutdown line. No warning or error.
- Latency is the closed-loop time of a mixed load that includes batches of up to 32 states on 4 CPU
  threads; the median 200 took 1.4 s in both the first and the last hour. These are stability
  numbers, not a speed benchmark.
